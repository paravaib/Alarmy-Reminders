1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

AlarmyReminders is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal personal information:

  • Alarm Data: Your alarm notes, scheduled times, and preferences are stored locally on your device
  • Usage Analytics: We may collect anonymous usage statistics to improve app performance
  • Subscription Status: Your subscription status is managed through Apple's App Store

1.2 Information We Don't Collect

We do not collect:

  • Your personal identity information (name, address, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Contact information from your device
  • Photos or media files
  • Web browsing history

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 Local Storage

All your alarm data is stored locally on your device using iOS's built-in storage mechanisms. This ensures your personal reminders remain private and secure.

2.2 Subscription Management

Subscription status is handled entirely through Apple's App Store and StoreKit framework. We do not store or process payment information.

2.3 App Improvement

We may use anonymous usage analytics to understand how the app is used and identify areas for improvement.

3. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

  • All alarm data is stored locally on your device
  • Data is encrypted using iOS's built-in encryption
  • No data is transmitted to external servers without your consent
  • We use secure connections (HTTPS) for any network communications

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 Apple Services

Our app integrates with Apple's services:

  • App Store: For app distribution and in-app purchases
  • StoreKit: For subscription management
  • AlarmKit: For alarm scheduling and notifications

Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for information about how Apple handles your data.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your alarm data (stored locally on your device)
  • Delete your alarm data (through the app's delete functionality)
  • Cancel your subscription (through Apple's App Store settings)
  • Contact us with privacy concerns at vaibhav@aayutech.in

6. Children's Privacy

AlarmyReminders is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by:

  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the bottom of this page
  • Providing notice through the app when significant changes are made
