🚀 Getting Started

Quick Start Guide

  1. Create Your First Alarm: Tap the "New Alarm Note" tab
  2. Write Your Note: Enter what you want to be reminded about
  3. Set the Time: Choose when you want to be notified
  4. Schedule: Tap "Create Alarm" to set your reminder

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How many alarms can I create with the free version?
The free version allows you to create up to 3 alarms per day. Your counter resets every 24 hours at midnight.
What happens when I upgrade to Pro?
Pro users get unlimited alarm creation and priority customer support. Your existing alarms remain unchanged.
How do I cancel my subscription?
You can cancel your subscription anytime through your iPhone Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions > AlarmyReminders.
Will my alarms work if I delete the app?
No, deleting the app will remove all your alarms. Make sure to export any important reminders before deleting.
Why isn't my alarm going off?
Make sure notifications are enabled for AlarmyReminders in Settings > Notifications. Also check that Do Not Disturb is not blocking notifications.

🔧 Troubleshooting

Common Issues & Solutions

🔕 Notifications Not Working

  • Check Settings > Notifications > AlarmyReminders is enabled
  • Ensure Do Not Disturb is not active
  • Verify your device is not in Silent mode
  • Restart the app and try creating a new alarm

📱 App Crashes or Freezes

  • Force close and reopen the app
  • Restart your iPhone
  • Update to the latest version from the App Store
  • Contact support if the issue persists

💳 Subscription Issues

  • Check your App Store account for payment issues
  • Verify subscription status in Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions
  • Contact Apple Support for billing problems
  • Email us for subscription-related app issues
